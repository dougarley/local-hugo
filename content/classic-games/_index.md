All walkup computers have classic emulators installed.

## ROMM

[ROMM](http://192.168.1.234:8090) is a self-hosted ROM library with **3,939 games** across 27 platforms, including Game Boy, GBA, N64, PlayStation, SNES, arcade, and more. It handles browsing, metadata, cover art, and launching games — all from the browser.

### Playing in the browser

Most platforms support in-browser emulation via EmulatorJS (WebAssembly — no installation needed). Click any game and hit **Play**. Handheld platforms (GB, GBC, GBA) and 16-bit consoles (SNES, Genesis) run well; heavier platforms like N64 and PS1 are playable but may vary by machine.

### Downloading ROMs

To take a game home and play it in your own emulator:

1. Open the game in ROMM
2. Click the **Download** button on the game page
3. The ROM file will save to your Downloads folder
